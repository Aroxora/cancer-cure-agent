PROTACs, or proteolysis-targeting chimeras, recruit the cell’s ubiquitin-proteasome machinery to selectively degrade target proteins, potentially allowing for the removal of intracellular proteins like LRG1. This approach has been explored as a complementary strategy to monoclonal antibodies for targeting extracellular proteins. Arvinus, known as the PROTAC company, received FDA approval for Vepistran, the first PROTAC drug, for second-line metastatic breast cancer with ESR1 mutations. This approval underscores the potential of PROTACs in cancer therapy, especially when resistance arises through target amplification or when selective degradation of mutant over wild-type proteins is needed.
